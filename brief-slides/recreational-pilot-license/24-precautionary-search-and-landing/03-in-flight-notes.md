# Language choices

> **"decide now"** — reinforces acting on the judgement, not just recognising it.

> **"keep the field in sight"** — repeated throughout the search and inspection.

> **"PAN early"** — say it as soon as the decision is made, not once you're committed.

# Before starting the plane

- Confirm the student's **fuel log**, **best-endurance calculations**, and the
  **pre-flight documents** (MEL, NOTAMs, GNSS RAIM, ERSA, AIP) for today's flight are
  complete and correct — assessed this lesson (C2.1, C4.2)
- Confirm **approved practice areas/fields** for today, and the operator's **minimum
  height for a go-around** from a simulated precautionary landing — brief this
  explicitly, it is never flown to an actual touchdown outside an approved area or
  aerodrome unless a genuine short-field landing there is part of your operator's
  procedures

# After departing / established in the climb

- Assess the **short-field take-off** on departure, if departing from a practice field

# Simulated precautionary landing — instructor demonstration

## Sequence
- In the training area, declare the scenario without warning: "Simulated deteriorating
  weather" (or "Simulated low fuel") — "Simulated precautionary landing"
- **Decide** — narrate the decision out loud, tied to the simulated trigger
- **Configure** — slow, safe cruise, some flap, appropriate height
- **Search** — fly toward the chosen area, narrating the criteria out loud
- **Select** — choose the field; point out approach/overshoot paths, length, surface
- **Inspect** — low pass at ~100 ft to the right of the landing path, climb to ~500 ft,
  circuit, dummy approach to the right, re-check
- **Communicate** — simulate the PAN call (do not transmit unless genuinely required)
- **Prepare** — narrate the passenger brief as height allows
- Fly the short-field landing, or go around no lower than the briefed minimum height

# Simulated precautionary landing — student flown

## Sequence
- Repeat from varying positions relative to the training area/approved fields, and with
  both simulated triggers (weather, low fuel)
- Student flies the full vital-actions sequence from memory: decide, configure, search,
  select, communicate, prepare, land
- Prompt only if a step is missed or out of order — let the sequence run uninterrupted
  otherwise
- Debrief briefly after each repetition: what worked, what would you do differently?

<!--

# Short-field landing — revision

## Sequence
- At the chosen/approved field: full flap, stable approach, aim point chosen short of
  obstacles
- Airspeed with elevator, descent rate with throttle — the standard approach split
- Aim to reach the minimum recommended speed crossing the boundary
- Minimal float, touch down precisely, then maximum braking without locking the wheels
- Go around without hesitation if the approach is unstable, too high, or too fast

<!--

Reinforce: "you cannot convert a bad short-field approach into a good landing."

# Other abnormal situations (simulated)

## Sequence
- Simulate a low-fuel situation or deteriorating weather distinct from the main
  exercise — e.g. a fuel-quantity discrepancy noticed in cruise, or a sudden reduction
  in visibility
- Student correctly identifies the situation and maintains safe control of the aeroplane
  throughout
- Student manages the situation in accordance with relevant procedures — which may mean
  triggering the full precautionary-landing sequence above, or a lesser response
  (diversion, altitude change) if appropriate

<!--

# Return to aerodrome

- Establish cruise on descent back toward the aerodrome
- **Listen to AWIS/ATIS** — runway in use, circuit direction, QNH
- Fly a normal circuit and landing
- Complete the post-flight actions and administration (C2.3) at shutdown
